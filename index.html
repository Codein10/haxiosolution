How We Built a Complete Digital Suite for Travkings Tours & Travels

The client

Travkings Tours and Travels Pvt. Ltd. is an IATA-accredited travel company with over 18 years of experience and branches in Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Nairobi, Dar es Salaam and Lubumbashi, serving travellers across India, Kenya, Tanzania and DR Congo.

The goal

Bring bookings, sales enquiries, payments and customer communication into one connected set of tools, so the team could work faster and travellers could stay in touch easily.

What we delivered

Four products designed, built and launched by Haxio Solution: an ERP for operations, a CRM for sales, the Smart Connect app for travellers and the company website, all working together.

How much does a website or app cost?+

It depends on the features, number of screens and integrations you need. After a free consultation we share a clear, fixed quote with no hidden costs, so you know the full price before we start.

How long does a project take?+

A business website usually takes 2–4 weeks. Mobile apps and custom software like ERP or CRM usually take 6–16 weeks, depending on scope. You get a timeline with milestones at the start.

Will I own the source code?+

Yes. Once the project is complete and paid, the full source code, designs and documentation are handed over to you.

Do you provide support after launch?+

Yes. Every project includes a support period after launch, and you can continue with our monthly support plan for updates, fixes and new features.

Can you work on my existing website or app?+

Yes. We can fix issues, improve speed and design, or add new features to software built by another team.

How will we communicate during the project?+

You talk directly with the people building your product on WhatsApp, calls or video meetings, with regular progress updates and demos.
